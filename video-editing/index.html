Questions

Video editing, answered.

How much does video editing cost?

Every edit is quoted on its length, the amount of footage, the complexity and the deadline. Send us a brief and we reply with a quote within one working day.

Do you edit Instagram Reels, TikToks and YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Short-form is a core part of our work. Send raw clips or a long video and we cut vertical 9:16 edits for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, one at a time or in batches.

How fast will I get my first cut?

A first cut usually arrives within 72 hours of us receiving your footage and brief. Tell us your deadline up front and we plan the edit around it.

How many revisions are included?

Two revision rounds are included in every edit. After that we deliver your final masters.

How do I send my footage?

Share it by Google Drive, Dropbox or Frame.io. A link is enough to get started.

Do you work with clients outside Egypt?

Yes. The work is fully remote. We edit for clients in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and the wider MENA region, and in the US, Canada and Europe.

What formats do you deliver?

Final masters in the aspect ratios and lengths you need: 16:9 for YouTube and web, 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, plus cutdowns for paid media.

Can you film the video as well?

Yes, in Egypt. Revframe is a full production studio in Cairo that films automotive launches, concerts, events, fashion and commercials. See our production work.
